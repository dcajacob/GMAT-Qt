HYG v3 star catalog attribution

inp_StarsHYGv3.txt is the unchanged OpenFramesInterface tabular subset of
David Nash / Astronexus's HYG v3 star database. Its fields are right ascension
(hours), declination (degrees), apparent magnitude, B-V color index and name.
The dataset is based on the Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese catalogs.
