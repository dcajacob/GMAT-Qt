Why E406 remains open
---------------------

The exact original row requires ephemeris only for the solution of iterative
processes. The two retained terminal cases provide a counterexample that cannot
be resolved by more passing format checks. Both outer DC and Yukon independently
require U=2. At their iteration limits they end FINISHED with native
EXCEEDED_ITERATIONS, U=1 and residual 1, while writing 25 OEM rows at radius
7,130 km. Their converged recoveries reach U=2 and radius 7,230 km.

The current EphemerisFile guide promises final-solution output; the Target
guide says last calculated controls can be applied after nonconvergence. It
does not establish that those controls satisfy the requested problem. Native
FINISHED and final-pass membership therefore do not prove the exact original
solution obligation. This review makes neither a convergence waiver nor a
new convergence-only product policy. E406 stays an explicit source/runtime
conflict pending a supported-behavior decision. The separate unconstrained
VF13ad accuracy failure remains open; it is not introduced as another E406
output-membership clause.
