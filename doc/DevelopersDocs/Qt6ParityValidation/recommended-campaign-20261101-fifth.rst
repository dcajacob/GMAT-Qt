Audit support and safeguards
-----------------------------

The recovery tool now handles two original receipt-index formats explicitly:
receipts hashed in the original audit's raw-artifact index, and receipts/claims
in an original hash-bound combined-evidence sidecar. A mapping without its own
authority table requires explicit review and exact source-row fingerprints plus
matching NASA authorities in every frozen execution inventory. An indexed claim
must bind the exact original row and complete mapped claim.

A ``missing_obligation`` decision can retain a failed binding as a diagnostic
with ``verified:false`` and an explicit rejection reason. It cannot supply a
pass. The campaign builder independently rejects any attempt to credit such a
check. Every accepted row still needs every complete passing mapped claim in
both modes; original receipts, mappings and assertions are unchanged.

The affected ``QtAcceptance.EvidenceRecovery`` registration passes25 tests:
the prior20 plus five new contracts for authority/index identities, original
raw-receipt membership, repeated-test claim completeness and rejection handling.
The existing union contract also rejects a diagnostic promoted into passing
credit. These support-test counts grant no NASA scope credit themselves.
